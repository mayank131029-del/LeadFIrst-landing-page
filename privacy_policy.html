Leadfirst ("Leadfirst", "we", "us", or "our") is a product/service operated by Techwix Private Limited ("Company"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website, applications, products, and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Leadfirst, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Company or business name
  • Billing information
  • Login credentials
  • Account and profile information

Lead and Form Data

Leadfirst is a lead collection and management platform. Information submitted by visitors through forms, landing pages, websites, or other connected sources may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Business information
  • Enquiry details
  • Form responses
  • Other information voluntarily submitted by the visitor

The customer using Leadfirst is responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis and required permissions to collect and process such lead information.

Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment gateways. We may receive transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction ID
  • Payment status
  • Amount paid
  • Payment date
  • Payment gateway reference

We generally do not directly store complete debit/credit card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, or other sensitive payment credentials.

Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Website usage information
  • Pages visited
  • Referring URLs
  • Log information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your Leadfirst account
  • Provide and operate our services
  • Process payments and subscriptions
  • Collect and manage leads

  • Provide customer support

The business or organization using Leadfirst is generally responsible for:
