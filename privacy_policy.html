Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

At LeadFirst Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("LeadFirst", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users and their clients. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your information when you use our website, dashboard, and API services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of personal information we collect include:

  • Account Information: When you sign up for LeadFirst, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and billing details.
  • Client & Lead Data: To provide our core services (lead collection and WhatsApp automation), we process data regarding your leads on your behalf. This includes lead names, contact details, form submissions, and UTM parameters.
  • Integration Data: Information retrieved from third-party services you authorize us to connect with (e.g., WordPress, Meta Ads, Razorpay).
  • Usage Information: We collect data regarding how you interact with our platform, such as login timestamps, feature usage, and IP addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services, including isolated workspaces for digital agencies.
  • To facilitate automated WhatsApp messaging sequences as configured by you.
  • To process payments securely through our partnered gateways (Razorpay and Cashfree).
  • To communicate with you regarding account updates, security alerts, and technical support.
  • To analyze usage trends and measure platform performance.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share information with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our platform (e.g., cloud hosting providers, WhatsApp Business API partners).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: If LeadFirst is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal data may be transferred.

4. Data Security & Isolation

Security is a core value at LeadFirst. We implement robust, industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration. For our Agency plan users, we enforce strict data isolation protocols ensuring that workspaces, client data, and campaigns are completely segregated and never mixed.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You may also have the right to request a portable copy of your data. You can manage most of this information directly within your LeadFirst Dashboard.

If you are processing lead data on behalf of your clients, you are responsible for ensuring that you have obtained the necessary consents required by applicable data protection laws before sending their data to our platform.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
