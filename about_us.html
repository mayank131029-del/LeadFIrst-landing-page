Our Story

Built by marketers, for marketers.

Before LeadFirst, we experienced the chaos of managing client leads firsthand. Leads were scattered across spreadsheets, email inboxes, and messy WhatsApp groups. Attribution was a nightmare, and response times were terrible.

We realized agencies didn't need another complex CRM. They needed a seamless bridge between a landing page, an automated WhatsApp response, and a payment gateway.

So, we built LeadFirst. An isolated, multi-tenant platform designed specifically to make agencies look brilliant in front of their clients.
